## Common questions

### Does my audio go to a server?

No. Transcription runs entirely on your device. You can turn on airplane mode and it still works. There is no SafeScribe server.

### Why is there a large download the first time?

Speech recognition needs a model file (about 874 MB). It downloads once and stays on your device. Use Wi‑Fi if you can — the app warns you before downloading over mobile data.

### The download failed or stopped partway.

Reopen **Settings → Transcription model** and start it again. Downloads resume where they left off; you do not pay for the same bytes twice. If it keeps failing, check that you have roughly 1 GB of free space.

### Do I need an account?

No. SafeScribe has no accounts, no sign-in and no password to lose.

### How long can a recording be?

The free version transcribes up to 10 minutes per file. SafeScribe Pro removes that limit. Every other feature is available in the free version.

### I bought Pro but it is not unlocked.

Open **Settings → SafeScribe Pro** and tap **Restore purchases**. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Note that a purchase is tied to your store account and does not transfer between the App Store and Google Play.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, under Apple's policy — request one through your App Store purchase history. We cannot issue refunds on Apple's behalf.

### The transcript has mistakes.

Automatic speech recognition is imperfect. Accuracy improves with a clearer recording: hold the phone closer to the speaker, reduce background noise, and avoid people talking over each other. Setting the audio language explicitly instead of auto-detect also helps.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete individual transcripts from the list, or remove everything at once from **Settings → Delete All Data**. Deleting the app also removes everything. We never hold a copy.

### Which languages are supported?

Transcription supports the languages of the underlying Whisper model — roughly 99, including Turkish and English. The app interface itself is in English and Turkish.

## Reporting a problem

If something is broken, please email us with:

1. What you did, and what happened instead
2. Whether it happens every time
3. The diagnostic report from **Settings → Share error log**
